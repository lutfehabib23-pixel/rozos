# breakout - the brick game (phase 18a)
  `breakout`  `breakout -f` (fps)  `breakout -p` (no menu)  `-s` (smooth)

Move the paddle under the ball and break every brick. The purple
(and in later levels the top) bricks take two hits. Where the ball
hits the paddle sets its angle; a moving paddle adds a little spin.
Each level is faster and has a different pattern; you have 3 balls.

  mouse, or arrows / A D   move the paddle
  Space or click           launch the ball
  P   pause       Esc   back to the menu

The background is a 1280x960 picture, smoothly shrunk once to the
screen size when the game starts (game_image_resize in [[rozgame]]).
Best score: /home/.rozgame/breakout.score.
Source: /usr/src/games/breakout.c - see [[games]].
